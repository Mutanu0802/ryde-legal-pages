2. Description of the Service

RYDE provides a digital platform through which passengers can request transportation and registered drivers can receive and fulfil ride requests.

The platform may enable users to:

  • Register and manage their accounts.
  • Request rides by selecting pickup and destination locations.
  • Select an available vehicle or service category.
  • Receive driver assignment and ride-status updates.
  • View available driver and vehicle information.
  • Track ride progress using the features available in the application.
  • Cancel rides in accordance with the applicable cancellation rules.
  • Complete rides and view relevant ride information.

RYDE's services and features may change as the platform develops.

RYDE facilitates the connection between passengers and drivers. The actual transportation service is provided by the driver. RYDE does not guarantee that a driver will always be available or that a ride will be completed within a particular time.

3. Eligibility and Account Registration

Users must provide accurate information during registration and maintain the accuracy of their account details.

3.1 Passengers

Passengers must:

  • Provide a valid name and telephone number and any other information required for registration.
  • Keep their login credentials confidential.
  • Provide accurate pickup and destination details.
  • Use the platform for lawful transportation purposes.
  • Treat drivers respectfully and follow reasonable safety instructions.

Users must meet the minimum age and legal capacity requirements applicable to the services under Kenyan law. Where a passenger is a minor, applicable legal requirements and any required parental or guardian arrangements must be satisfied.

3.2 Drivers

Drivers must:

  • Provide accurate personal and contact information.
  • Hold a valid driving licence appropriate to the vehicle operated.
  • Maintain all legally required vehicle licences, inspections, insurance and permits.
  • Provide accurate vehicle details and registration information.
  • Meet RYDE's applicable driver approval and verification requirements.
  • Keep their vehicle roadworthy, clean and safe for passengers.
  • Comply with applicable traffic, road safety and transport laws.
  • Keep their driver account secure and their availability status accurate.

Drivers must not accept rides while unlicensed, impaired, unavailable or otherwise unable to transport passengers safely and lawfully.

3.3 Account Security

Each user is responsible for protecting their login credentials and promptly notifying RYDE if they suspect unauthorised access to their account.

Users must not impersonate another person, create fraudulent accounts, or share accounts in a way that compromises platform safety or security.

4. Booking and Driver Assignment

Passengers may request a ride by selecting the required pickup location, destination and available service category.

RYDE may automatically assign a suitable available driver based on factors such as vehicle category, driver availability and operational requirements.

A ride request is subject to driver availability and successful processing by the platform. A request does not guarantee that a driver will be assigned.

Once a driver is assigned, the passenger may be shown relevant information, including the driver's name, contact number, vehicle description, registration plate and ride status, where supported by the application.

Passengers must verify that the arriving vehicle and driver correspond to the information displayed in the application before beginning the journey.

Drivers must only accept or undertake rides assigned or otherwise authorised through the platform, subject to the functionality available in their application.

5. Fares, Charges and Payment

The applicable fare and any additional charges must be communicated to the passenger through the platform or otherwise agreed before the ride begins, where practicable.

Fares may depend on factors such as the selected vehicle category, journey distance, applicable pricing settings and other disclosed charges.

Passengers agree to pay the applicable fare for completed rides using the payment method or arrangement supported by RYDE at the time of the ride.

Where a fare estimate is provided, it may differ from the final fare if the actual journey or applicable pricing conditions differ from the estimate. Any adjustments must be consistent with the information disclosed to the passenger and applicable law.

RYDE will communicate any applicable platform fees, cancellation charges or other additional charges before they are imposed, where required.

Passengers must not evade legitimate ride charges, and drivers must not demand undisclosed payments or misrepresent the fare.

Refunds, disputed fares and payment adjustments will be reviewed according to the circumstances, applicable platform policies and Kenyan law.

6. Ride Cancellations and Failed Trips

Passengers may cancel ride requests using the cancellation feature available in the application.

Drivers must promptly update the ride status when a journey is cancelled, a passenger has been picked up, or a journey has otherwise progressed to a different stage.

Cancellation charges, if introduced or applicable, must be disclosed to users before they are imposed.

A cancellation request may not automatically terminate a ride if the application has failed to process it. Users should verify the ride status and contact RYDE support when necessary.

RYDE may investigate repeated cancellations, false ride requests, deliberate no-shows or other conduct that disrupts the service.

Any cancellation charge, refund or adjustment must be handled in accordance with the applicable policy and law.

7. Passenger Responsibilities

Passengers agree to:

  • Enter accurate pickup and destination details.
  • Be reasonably ready at the agreed pickup point.
  • Verify the driver's identity and vehicle details before entering the vehicle.
  • Wear seat belts where provided and comply with applicable safety requirements.
  • Treat drivers respectfully and refrain from abusive, threatening, discriminatory or violent conduct.
  • Avoid damaging vehicles or interfering with safe driving.
  • Refrain from requesting unlawful transportation activities.
  • Report safety concerns, misconduct or suspicious activity to RYDE.

Passengers must not use the platform to transport prohibited items, commit offences, harass drivers or expose others to unreasonable safety risks.

8. Driver Responsibilities

Drivers agree to:

  • Transport passengers safely, professionally and lawfully.
  • Follow applicable road traffic rules and speed limits.
  • Maintain a valid driving licence and required vehicle documentation.
  • Keep vehicle information accurate and up to date.
  • Confirm the correct passenger and destination before starting the journey.
  • Update ride progress accurately in the Driver application.
  • Avoid discrimination, harassment, intimidation and inappropriate conduct.
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol, drugs or other impairing substances.
  • Never use a mobile phone in a manner that compromises safe driving.
  • Protect passenger contact details and other personal information.
  • Report accidents, safety incidents, suspected fraud or serious misconduct promptly.

Drivers must not substitute an unauthorised driver or vehicle for the one registered and approved on the platform.

9. Ride Completion and Status Updates

Drivers are responsible for updating ride statuses accurately as the journey progresses, including arrival, pickup, journey commencement and completion, where these features are available.

Passengers should confirm that the journey has ended before treating the ride as complete.

RYDE may use ride-status information to maintain ride records, manage driver availability, address disputes and improve platform operations.

Users must not falsely mark rides as completed, fabricate journey records or manipulate ride statuses.

Users should verify vehicle and driver details, avoid entering vehicles that do not match the booking, and report suspicious behaviour.

In the event of an immediate threat to life or safety, users should contact the appropriate emergency services or police without waiting for a response from RYDE.

Users should report accidents, harassment, unsafe driving, theft or other serious incidents to RYDE as soon as reasonably possible.

RYDE may investigate reported incidents and take appropriate action, including suspending access to the platform. RYDE cannot guarantee the conduct of every user or prevent every incident.
