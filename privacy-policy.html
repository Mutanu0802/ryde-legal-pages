01

Introduction

RYDE (“RYDE,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a ride-hailing platform connecting passengers with registered drivers in Kitui County, Kenya. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, retain, and protect personal information through the RYDE Passenger application, RYDE Driver application, and related services.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Kenyan law, including the Data Protection Act, 2019. Where RYDE determines the purposes and means of processing, RYDE acts as a data controller. This policy should be read alongside our Terms of Service. Using RYDE acknowledges these practices; where consent is legally required, we request it separately.

02

Information We Collect

Passenger information

Name, phone number, optional email and profile details; pickup and destination locations; ride bookings and histories; applicable payment and transaction details; support requests and complaints.

Driver information

Name and contact details; licence and verification records; vehicle registration, category, model, insurance and inspection details; availability, assigned rides, and ride histories.

Location information

Pickup and destination locations; driver location while available for assignments; and location or route updates during active rides where supported.

Ride and transaction information

Ride requests, assignments, cancellations, trip status, fare estimates, final charges, payment confirmations, transaction references, refunds and disputes where applicable.

Device and technical information

Device type, operating system, app version, IP address, login and security records, device identifiers where applicable, diagnostic information and error logs.

03

How We Use Personal Information

Accounts and access

Create accounts, authenticate users, manage profiles and access permissions.

Ride services

Match passengers with available drivers, coordinate pickup and destinations, provide ride status and relevant driver and vehicle information.

Payments

Record applicable fares, confirm transactions, resolve disputes and administer refunds or adjustments where supported.

Safety and support

Verify driver eligibility, prevent fraud, investigate incidents, respond to complaints and secure the platform.

Improvement and compliance

Troubleshoot issues, analyse service performance (using aggregated or anonymised information where practicable) and comply with legal obligations.

04

Legal Bases for Processing

Contract

To deliver the services users request, including account management, ride coordination and relevant payments.

Legitimate interests

For proportionate security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and platform improvement, subject to users’ rights.

Legal obligations

To comply with applicable legal, financial, regulatory and transport requirements.

Consent

For activities that require permission, including certain optional communications or analytics. Consent may be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing.

05

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell users’ personal information. We share only information reasonably necessary for the purposes described here.

Passengers and drivers

Assigned drivers may receive a passenger’s name, contact information, pickup point and destination. Passengers may receive a driver’s name, contact number, vehicle description, registration plate and ride status.

Service providers

Hosting, authentication, mapping, payment, communications, security and technical service providers may process necessary information under appropriate safeguards.

Authorities and safety

We may disclose information to lawful authorities or where permitted by law to address serious safety incidents, suspected misconduct or legal obligations.

Users must not misuse personal information received through the platform.

06

Location Data and Privacy

RYDE uses location data to help identify pickup and destination points, match drivers, coordinate rides and display journey progress where supported. We request device location permissions where required.

Users can change device location permissions in their settings, but disabling access may affect ride-booking, assignment and tracking features. If background location collection is introduced, RYDE will provide the relevant disclosure and seek permissions required by law and platform rules.

We do not disclose user location to unrelated third parties for unrelated advertising or tracking without an appropriate lawful basis.

07

Data Storage and Security

RYDE aims to implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, such as account authentication, role-based access controls, secure communications, restricted database access, backups and security maintenance, as applicable to the deployed system.

No electronic system is entirely secure. Users should safeguard login credentials and report suspected unauthorised access. We will assess and respond to personal data breaches in accordance with applicable Kenyan requirements.

08

International Data Transfers

Some technology providers may process or store information outside Kenya. Where international transfers occur, RYDE will seek appropriate safeguards and lawful transfer mechanisms under Kenyan data protection law.

09

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for account administration, rides, transactions, safety, fraud prevention, disputes and legal obligations. Specific retention periods depend on the data category and applicable requirements.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it. Some records may remain after account closure where legally required or otherwise lawfully justified.

10

Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to their personal information; correction of inaccurate or incomplete data; deletion where legally appropriate; objection to certain processing; and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. Other rights may apply, including certain rights concerning automated decisions and data portability.

To make a request, email jmutanuben@gmail.com. We may reasonably verify your identity before disclosing or changing information. Users may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya.

11

Cookies, Analytics and App Technologies

RYDE websites and related platforms may use cookies or similar technologies, where implemented, for sign-in, security, preferences, diagnostics and usage analysis. Non-essential technologies requiring consent will be enabled only with the necessary permission.

Browser or app settings may offer controls. Disabling essential technologies can affect core functionality.

12

Children’s Privacy

Users must satisfy the age and legal capacity requirements applicable to RYDE services in Kenya. Where a passenger is a minor, legally required parental or guardian arrangements must be satisfied.

If we discover that children’s personal information was processed without an appropriate lawful basis or authorisation, we will take appropriate corrective steps. Parents and guardians may contact us with concerns.

13

Account Deletion and Data Removal

Users may request account closure and deletion by emailing jmutanuben@gmail.com. After verifying a valid request, RYDE will close or deactivate the account and delete information that no longer needs to be retained.

Certain transaction, security or dispute records may be retained where legally required or otherwise justified, and will remain protected.

14

Third-Party Services and Links

RYDE may integrate with mapping, payments, communications, hosting or other external providers. Their own privacy notices may apply when users interact with them directly. We aim to apply appropriate safeguards when providers process data on our behalf.

15

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology, security practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be made available through RYDE’s official channels with a revised effective date. We will provide notice or obtain consent where required.

16

Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Kenya, including the Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable regulations. Nothing here limits users’ statutory rights or remedies.
